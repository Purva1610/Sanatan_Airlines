Sanatan coordinates Tirth Yatra enquiries — not fixed tours. We listen to what matters: the shrine you hold close, the people travelling with you, and the pace your body can keep. Then we help shape a journey through partners we trust.

Nothing on this page is a booking. Darshan timings, temple access and road conditions rest with temple authorities and local conditions. What we offer is careful preparation, honest guidance and a coordinator who stays with the details.

How it works

Four quiet steps,
no pressure.

From first curiosity to a coordinated journey, the pace stays yours. An enquiry does not reserve a place or confirm dates.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Wander through the four paths and the suggested journeys. Nothing is reserved, and nothing is rushed.

  2. 02

    Enquire

    Share the path, season and group you have in mind. A short enquiry, with no payment and no obligation.

  3. 03

    Personal Guidance

    A coordinator reviews your enquiry and speaks with you about what may be possible — honestly, and in confidence.

  4. 04

    Coordinated Journey

    If the path feels right, we coordinate stays, transfers and local guidance through our partners, subject to availability and temple schedules.
