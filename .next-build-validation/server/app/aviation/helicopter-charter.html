Good to know

Before you enquire.

Can any location be used as a landing site?+

No. Every landing location is reviewed individually. Permissions, surface conditions, surrounding obstructions and local rules must be confirmed before a site can be considered.

Is weather a factor?+

Yes. Helicopter operations depend on weather and daylight. A coordinator will discuss feasible windows with the operator for your dates.

Does this confirm a helicopter for my date?+

No. This is an enquiry only. Operator availability, feasibility and terms are confirmed separately.

What about pilgrimage support?+

We coordinate transfers around yatra itineraries, subject to landing permissions, weather and licensed operator availability.
