Hotel + Flight FAQs

Can I change just the hotel or just the flight in a package?

Packages are sold as a single product. Changes to either component affect the full package pricing. Speak to our holidays team for change requests.

Are SkyNova Miles earned on hotel stays in a package?

Yes — flights in a hotel+flight package earn SkyNova Miles at the standard rate. Hotel stays also earn partner points if the hotel is a SkyNova partner.

What is the cancellation policy for packages?

Package cancellation terms depend on the components. The flight follows standard fare rules; the hotel follows the hotel's own cancellation policy. We show combined cancellation terms clearly at checkout.

Can I add activities or transfers?

Yes — airport transfers and selected city activities can be added to packages during or after booking, subject to availability at the destination.
