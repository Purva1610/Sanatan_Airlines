Cabin Baggage Rules

All cabin bags must fit in the overhead locker or under the seat in front. Maximum dimensions are 56 cm × 45 cm × 25 cm (sum of dimensions: 126 cm). Bags that exceed these measurements will be checked at the gate.

In addition to your main cabin bag, you may carry one personal item: a handbag, laptop bag or small backpack not exceeding 40 cm × 30 cm × 15 cm.

  • All items must be securely stowed before takeoff
  • Cabin bag weight limits are strictly enforced at select airports
  • Musical instruments under 10 kg may be carried in cabin — see our policy
  • Duty-free bags from airport shops are permitted in addition to your allowance
Restricted Items

What Cannot Travel on Board

⚠️

Power banks / lithium batteries

Cabin only. Max 100Wh (20,000 mAh). Spare batteries in cargo strictly prohibited.

⚠️

Sharp objects (scissors, knives)

Must be in checked baggage. Blades over 6 cm prohibited in cabin.

⚠️

Liquids in cabin

Max 100 ml per container in a transparent, resealable 1-litre bag. One bag per passenger.

⚠️

Flammable materials

Completely prohibited on all Sanatan Airlines flights — cabin and cargo.

⚠️

Firearms

Declared sporting firearms may travel in checked baggage subject to strict documentation and prior approval.

⚠️

Pressurised spray cans

Max 500 ml per item, max 2 kg aggregate. Personal care only. Must not be in hold cargo.

Baggage FAQs

Can I pool baggage allowances across passengers on the same booking?

Yes — pooling is permitted for passengers on the same booking, same flight and same cabin class. Total pooled weight cannot exceed 32 kg per piece.

What happens if my bag is overweight?

Overweight bags (above the declared weight) are charged at the airport overweight rate, which is significantly higher than pre-purchased rates.

How is my baggage tracked?

All checked baggage is tagged with a barcode. You can track your bag via Manage Booking from the moment it is checked in until collection at the destination carousel.

What do I do if my bag is damaged?

Report damaged baggage immediately at the Baggage Services desk before leaving the airport. Claims submitted after leaving the terminal may not be accepted.

Are lithium batteries allowed in checked bags?

Spare lithium batteries and power banks must travel in cabin baggage only. Installed batteries in devices (laptops, phones) may travel in hold. No battery over 160 Wh is permitted on any Sanatan Airlines flight.
