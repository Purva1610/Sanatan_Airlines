Booking FAQs

Can I book a flight for someone else?

Yes. Enter the passenger's details (name as on passport, date of birth) during booking. The booking confirmation will be sent to the email address you provide.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay, UPI, Net Banking, and SkyNova Miles (full or partial payment for eligible bookings).

When do I receive my ticket?

Your e-ticket is emailed within 5 minutes of a successful payment. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or retrieve your booking with the PNR.

Is there a booking fee?

No booking fee is charged for payments by UPI, Net Banking or Debit Card. A 1.8% convenience fee applies to Credit Card payments.

What if two passengers have the same name?

Each passenger is a separate profile. During booking, enter each person's full name, date of birth and passport/ID details individually.

Can I book a one-way international ticket?

Yes. One-way international tickets are fully available. Note that some countries may require proof of onward travel for visa or immigration purposes.
