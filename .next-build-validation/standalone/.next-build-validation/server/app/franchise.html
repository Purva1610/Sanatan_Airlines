All franchise FAQs
Who is the franchise intended for?+

The concept is aimed at experienced, service-minded applicants with local market knowledge and the ability to meet responsibilities defined in a final agreement. Formal eligibility criteria are pending approval.

Is a territory exclusive?+

No territory model or exclusivity has been approved. Any location, territory rights or restrictions must be stated in a written agreement.

What investment is required?+

No investment amount is published. The refundable security deposit and setup or infrastructure expenditure are separate components, and their amounts and conditions remain pending approval.

Are revenue, profit, ROI or payback guaranteed?+

No. No earnings or return is guaranteed. Any future figures must be approved and are illustrative only.
