All membership FAQs
What is the International Club?+

It is a membership concept for international travel planning and personal coordination. Specific services, eligibility and benefits are still subject to approval.

What does a plan include?+

Final inclusions have not been approved. The published plan terms will identify each included service and any third-party costs or exclusions.

How long is membership valid?+

The validity period is not finalized. It will be stated in the approved terms before membership enquiries can become commitments.

Which destinations or countries are included?+

No destination entitlement is currently published. The final terms must define eligible destinations and exactly how a “country” or “visit” is counted.

Are flights, accommodation, meals or transport included?+

These items are not confirmed as inclusions. Do not assume they are included unless final approved plan terms explicitly say so.
