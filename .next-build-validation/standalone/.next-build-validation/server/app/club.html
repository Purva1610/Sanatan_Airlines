Membership terms

Clear terms,
before you decide.

These notes explain the boundaries of a coordination membership. They are a summary, not the final membership agreement.

Membership terms status
What membership includes+

Membership provides personalized travel coordination and guidance. The final scope of services is shared in writing during your consultation, before any commitment is made.

Coordination, not a fixed package+

Sanatan coordinates with partner airlines, hotels, transport providers and destination specialists. Travel services are delivered by those partners and remain subject to their own terms and availability.

Availability and confirmation+

No destination, date, itinerary or inclusion is guaranteed by this page. Every journey is confirmed individually in writing, subject to partner availability and final confirmation.

Partner networks+

Our partner network evolves over time. Where a particular partner or arrangement is unavailable, we will discuss suitable alternatives with you.

Travel documents, health and insurance+

Travellers remain responsible for valid passports, visas, vaccinations and adequate travel insurance. We can offer guidance, but approvals rest with the relevant authorities and providers.

Changes, cancellation and privacy+

Changes and cancellations follow the terms of the relevant partners and are explained before confirmation. Enquiry details are handled confidentially under our Privacy Policy.

All membership FAQs
How do I join the Sanatan International Club?+

Begin with a membership enquiry. A coordinator will speak with you about the formats, what coordination includes and what it does not, before anything is decided.

Is the club a fixed package?+

No. Membership is a personalized coordination service. Journeys are planned around each member and confirmed individually.

Are prices shown on this website?+

No. Membership terms are discussed personally during your consultation, and no payment is taken on this site.

Can you arrange pilgrimage journeys?+

Yes. Pilgrimage guidance sits at the heart of the club, including Kashi, Ayodhya, Char Dham, Tirupati, Shirdi and destinations abroad, subject to availability.

Do you guarantee a destination or specific dates?+

No. Requests are coordinated with partner networks, but availability, dates and itineraries are confirmed only for your specific journey.

Is membership suitable for a single traveller?+

Yes. The Single format is designed for individual seekers and receives the same personal coordination as every other format.
