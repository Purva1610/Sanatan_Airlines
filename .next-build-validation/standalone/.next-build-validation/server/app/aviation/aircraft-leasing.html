The service

An independent view before a long-term decision.

Acquiring, selling or leasing an aircraft is a long-term commitment. The value of good advice is measured in the questions asked early.

Sanatan Aviation provides coordination and advisory support — helping you define requirements, understand options and engage verified third-party partners. We do not own, buy or sell aircraft as principal, and we do not operate aircraft.
