The service

Calm coordination when every hour matters.

Medical flights are arranged around a patient, a clinical team and a receiving hospital — not around a schedule.

Sanatan Aviation coordinates non-emergency and time-sensitive enquiries with licensed air ambulance operators. Clinical suitability, aircraft configuration, medical escort requirements and ground transfers are confirmed by the relevant medical and operating teams before any service proceeds.

Round-the-clock enquiry line

A coordinator can receive time-sensitive requests at any hour. Emergencies must go to local emergency services.

Medical configuration

Stretcher systems, oxygen and escort requirements are matched with operator capability and clinical advice.

Family accompaniment

Where the cabin configuration allows, seating and updates for accompanying family are coordinated with care.

Ground transfer alignment

Ambulance and hospital-side timing are aligned where possible so the handover is calm and clear.

How coordination works

From first question to informed next steps.

Four clear stages. No pressure, no hidden assumptions — only what can genuinely be confirmed.

  1. 01

    Share the essentials

    Origin, destination, patient condition category and timing — high-level details only.

  2. 02

    Engage operators

    Licensed air ambulance operators are approached with the route and clinical requirements.

  3. 03

    Confirm suitability

    Medical team, aircraft configuration and availability are confirmed together.

  4. 04

    Align the handover

    Ground transfer and receiving-team timing are coordinated before the flight proceeds.

Good to know

Before you enquire.

Can I use this form in a medical emergency?+

No. This is not an emergency dispatch channel. Call your local emergency services immediately. This form is for non-emergency and time-sensitive planning enquiries.

Does submitting confirm a medical flight?+

No. Clinical suitability, operator capability, aircraft availability and terms must all be confirmed first.

What medical information should I share?+

Only the high-level details requested. A relevant medical team can advise what else is needed through an appropriate channel. Please do not send records through this form.

Can family travel with the patient?+

Where the aircraft configuration allows, family accompaniment can be coordinated. This is confirmed case by case with the operator and medical team.
