ABOUT SANATAN

Three connected service areas. One clear approach.

Sanatan Airlines Private Limited brings together aviation facilitation, international travel membership and global business-service enquiries. Each service starts with a clear brief and transparent next steps.

Aviation

Private jet charter facilitation, helicopter coordination, air ambulance coordination, aircraft advisory, leasing and aviation concierge enquiries. Flights are subject to licensed operator confirmation.

International Club

A developing membership concept for international travel planning. Eligibility, price, entitlements and benefits remain subject to approved terms.

Global Network

Information and enquiries across tourism, education, employment assistance, business, property, investment introductions, PR and trade. Regulated service delivery belongs to authorized providers.
