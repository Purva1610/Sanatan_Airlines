Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 20 November 2026

⚠ This document is prepared for legal review before publication. Please consult qualified legal counsel before making it publicly available.

This Cookie Policy explains how Sanatan Airlines Private Limited uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and mobile application. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy. By continuing to use our website after reviewing this policy, you consent to our use of cookies as described below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device on subsequent visits and to remember your preferences, login status and behaviour. Cookies cannot execute programs or deliver viruses to your device.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They enable core features such as security, session management and booking flow continuity. You cannot opt out of strictly necessary cookies as the website cannot work properly without them.

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve page content, navigation and booking flows. Data collected is aggregated and anonymised.

2.4 Marketing & Targeting Cookies

With your consent, we place marketing cookies to show relevant advertisements on third-party platforms and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. These cookies may track your activity across other websites.

3. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Browser instructions for managing cookies:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You may also opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or applicable regulations change. We will notify you of significant changes by displaying a notice on our website. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance.
