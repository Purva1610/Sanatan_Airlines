Grievance Redressal

Last Updated: 20 November 2026

⚠ This document is prepared for legal review before publication. Please consult qualified legal counsel before making it publicly available.

Sanatan Airlines Private Limited is committed to addressing passenger grievances promptly, transparently and in full compliance with the DGCA Passenger Charter, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the Information Technology Act, 2000. This page sets out our grievance channels, timelines and escalation process.

2. How to File a Complaint

Step 1 — First Contact

Contact our Customer Service team first. Most issues are resolved at this stage.

  • Online: Use the Contact Us page complaint form
  • Email: support@sanatanairlines.com
  • Phone: 1800 123 4567 (Toll-free) | +91 231 123 4567 (International)
  • WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210
  • Airport customer service desk: Available at all Sanatan Airlines operated stations

Resolution timeline: Simple complaints — within 24 hours. Complex matters — within 7 working days.

Step 2 — Escalation to Grievance Officer

If you are not satisfied with the first-contact resolution (or if 7 working days have elapsed without resolution), escalate to the Grievance Officer at grievance@sanatanairlines.com with:

  • Your booking reference number (PNR)
  • Your full name and contact details
  • Details of the original complaint and earlier reference number if any
  • Supporting documents (boarding pass, receipts, medical certificates etc.)

Resolution timeline: Acknowledgement within 48 hours. Resolution within 15 working days.

Step 3 — External Escalation

If your complaint remains unresolved after Step 2, you may escalate to:

  • DGCA Consumer Facilitation Centre: File online at dgca.gov.in or by post to DGCA Headquarters, Opposite Safdarjung Airport, New Delhi – 110003.
  • Consumer Forum: Approach the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 at the forum having jurisdiction over your place of residence or the booked origin station.
  • Airline Dispute Resolution (ADR): If notified by DGCA in future, Sanatan Airlines will participate in the designated ADR scheme.

3. Categories of Grievances We Handle

  • Flight delays, cancellations and diversions
  • Denied boarding and overbooking
  • Baggage damage, loss or delay
  • Refund disputes and processing delays
  • Special assistance failures
  • Discriminatory or disrespectful treatment by staff
  • Ticket fare disputes and ancillary charge disputes
  • Data privacy complaints
  • Website or application technical failures affecting booking
  • SkyNova Rewards miles disputes

5. IT Grievance Officer (Under IT Act / IT Rules)

In accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, our Grievance Officer for IT and digital matters is reachable at grievance@sanatanairlines.com. Complaints regarding content, data or digital services will be acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days.
