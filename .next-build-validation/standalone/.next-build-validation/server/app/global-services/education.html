05 / FAQs

Clear answers
for this service.

Can you guarantee admission?+

No. Admission decisions are made by each institution and depend on its requirements and assessment.

Can you guarantee a student visa?+

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant government authority. Check official requirements for your circumstances.

Will institution partners be named?+

Institution or partner names will be provided only where the details are approved for disclosure.

Do you make admission decisions?+

No. Sanatan can coordinate guidance and documentation discussions; institutions assess applications and set their own requirements.
